    Solo · design + build · 140 of 140 commits

    One booking engine for any airline, built by AI agents — a harness of 5 agents and 32 skills — without Figma. It began with research into competitor airlines, platforms and UX patterns. The front end talks to any airline's backend through one contract: it asks which of 28 capabilities the backend can serve and hides what it can't. Each airline picks its components in the admin, and its whole style is one JSON — a new airline in a day. The next step is the main idea: a front end that adapts to each passenger, composed from Paximizer CDP advice — the order of steps, the offers, the layout — with the total price always in view.
